Overview

HabiTurn is a habit-tracking app built with one core belief: your personal data belongs to you alone. We do not operate any servers that store your data, we do not collect analytics, and we do not serve ads. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data and who can access it.

Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you create in HabiTurn is stored locally on your device using SQLite: • Habit names, descriptions, colors, and schedules. • Daily completion logs and progress values. • Your display name and chosen avatar. • Notification reminder times. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up. No one — including us — can access your local data.

Cloud Backup — Google Drive & iCloud

HabiTurn offers an optional backup feature. If you choose to enable it: • On Android, your backup is saved directly to your personal Google Drive account. • On iOS, your backup is saved directly to your personal iCloud account. In both cases, the backup file is stored in your own cloud storage — exactly like any other file you save to Drive or iCloud. HabiTurn has no servers and never receives or holds a copy of your backup. Your cloud backup is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (for Drive) or Apple's Privacy Policy (for iCloud), both of which provide strong security and privacy protections. You can review or delete your backup file at any time directly in Google Drive or iCloud. Cloud backup is entirely optional — the app is fully functional without it.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We collect absolutely nothing from you. Specifically: • No account registration or login is required to use HabiTurn. • No usage analytics or crash reports are sent to us. • No advertising identifiers or device fingerprints are collected. • No location data is accessed. • No contacts, camera, or microphone access is requested. • No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. HabiTurn makes no network requests of its own. The only internet access used by the app is the standard Google Drive or iCloud SDK when you initiate a backup — and that goes directly to your account.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, HabiTurn schedules reminders locally on your device for the habit times you set. These are standard local notifications — they are processed entirely on-device and no notification content or schedule is transmitted outside your device.

Permissions We Request

HabiTurn may request the following device permissions: • Notifications — to send you habit reminders at the times you choose. You can disable this any time in device settings. • Storage / Files (Android) — only used to write your backup file to your Google Drive account if you enable backup. • iCloud (iOS) — only used to write your backup file to your iCloud account if you enable backup. No other permissions are requested or used.

Deleting Your Data

You have full control over your data at all times: • Delete all local data: Settings → Danger Zone → Clear All Data. • Delete your cloud backup: open Google Drive or iCloud on your device and delete the HabiTurn backup file directly. • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data from your device. Since we hold no copies of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

Children's Privacy

HabiTurn does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because all data is stored locally and we collect nothing, the app is safe for all ages. If you have any concerns, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this screen. For significant changes we will display a notice inside the app. Continued use of HabiTurn after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
